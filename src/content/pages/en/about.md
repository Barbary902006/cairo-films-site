Moustafa Elbarbari is a film producer and the founder of Cairo Films, an independent production company focused on impact-driven cinema at the intersection of social issues and independent film. He works between Toronto and Egypt.

He is Senior Manager of Market Sales at TIFF: The Market. On set, he has worked as an assistant director through DGC Ontario on scripted Canadian television, and he holds a Master of Digital Media from Toronto Metropolitan University.

His training in the international film business includes the 2019 Beirut–Locarno Industry Academy International, a programme covering sales, marketing, distribution and programming. He was also selected for the inaugural edition of the Impact Lab in Beirut, which brought together a first generation of impact producers.

Before moving into film, Moustafa spent eight years in the corporate sector at Procter & Gamble, HSBC and Etisalat.

His current slate includes the short documentary *Occupy Toronto*, directed by Yousef Adris, and the short fiction film *What I Owe to My Mother*, directed by Khaled Medhat and co-produced with Supernova Films.
